A-Game -- made for you.

Windows: double-click A-Game.exe. The first time, Windows may say
  "Windows protected your PC" -> click "More info" -> "Run anyway". (It's safe;
  it's just not signed.)
Mac: open the .dmg, then drag A-Game to Applications (or just double-click it).
  If it's blocked, right-click the app -> Open, then confirm.
Controls: WASD or arrow keys to move, E to interact, Enter/Space to advance
  dialogue, M to mute, F11 for fullscreen.
Best played in one sitting, with sound on.
